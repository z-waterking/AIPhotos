Create one original poetic Chinese landscape painting about an unfulfilled promise to live quietly together tending cattle and sheep on the northern frontier. The promised peaceful pastoral life is close enough to touch, yet the other person is absent. One unified landscape composition, native 1536x1024, entirely wordless.

Scene and composition: an immense, quiet autumn steppe at dusk beyond the northern frontier, with pale ochre grasses, a winding shallow stream and layered distant blue-gray ridges. The land and open sky occupy most of the image. At the lower-left edge, show the corner of a small intact rustic pastoral shelter made of rough stone and weathered wood, homely rather than ruined. At its open threshold ONE fictional adult man in his thirties sits on a plain low wooden stool, back and slight side toward the viewer, quietly looking across the pasture. He has ordinary adult proportions, dark hair loosely tied back, a simple coarse brown-gray travel robe, opaque full clothing and a calm still posture. He is a small supporting figure in the expansive landscape, about six percent of the canvas area, neither a heroic portrait nor a dramatic performer. His hands rest naturally.

Beside him, at the same scale and facing the same distant view, place a SECOND plainly visible wooden stool that is EMPTY. Let its empty space read clearly, softly touched by the last light. This one unoccupied seat is the sole intimate visual turn: the ordinary life once imagined for two now has only one person. No second person, transparent figure, ghost, memory montage or inset scene.

In the middle distance, a small grouping of three sturdy brown cattle and roughly a dozen cream-white sheep grazes and slowly returns toward a simple short wooden enclosure. Distinct believable cow and sheep silhouettes, sensible legs, grounded bodies, soft long shadows, modest spacing; no animal is interacting with the absent seat. The cattle and sheep must be visibly recognizable, important enough to read as the promised pastoral livelihood. A sparse fence line gently leads the eye away from the vacant seat into the herd and the open land. Leave wide quiet stretches of grass between foreground, animals and distant ridges, so the space itself carries the longing.

Medium: a richly hand-painted Chinese poetic illustration combining sensitive ink contours, layered mineral pigment and matte gouache washes on slightly warm textured silk. Bold but restrained painterly strokes in the grass and wooden shelter; delicate broad translucent sky washes; clear intimate detail only at the two seats. Beautiful mature painting, without photographic skin or 3D rendering. Original composition rather than a recreation of a film still, existing illustration, named character portrait or published cover.

Warm dry grasses, muted cream, quiet ochre, slate blue and gentle smoke-gray. The upper sky gradually cools into soft open evening haze. Spacious and tender, deeply wistful, restrained rather than melodramatic. A peaceful home is possible here, but the promise remains unanswered.
