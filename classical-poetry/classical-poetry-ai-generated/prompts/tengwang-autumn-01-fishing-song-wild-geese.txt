Create one original wordless Chinese poetic landscape painting inspired by two parallel clauses from Wang Bo's public-domain Preface to the Pavilion of Prince Teng: fishing boats singing at dusk, their songs carried across the shores of Lake Pengli; a formation of wild geese feeling the autumn chill, their calls fading over the waters near Hengyang. This is a lyrical synthesis of distant places, not a literal geographic map. Make the scene emotionally immediate and visually unified.

Scene and story: a very broad, tranquil southern Chinese lake in late autumn, just after sunset. In the lower-left foreground, one modest old wooden fishing skiff returns along a soft bend of water. Its fully clothed fictional adult fisherman sits securely near the stern, lightly holding a single oar; head lifted slightly and mouth subtly open as if singing an unhurried evening song. He is a small lived-in presence, not a portrait. A second much smaller boat is far across the water, a tiny muted amber lamp marking its return home. Nets are folded simply aboard; no caught animals need be visible. A few dark tawny reeds and an irregular quiet shore anchor the near left corner.

In the upper-right open sky, a loose natural V of nine or eleven recognizable wild geese flies away toward the southern distance. Show varied believable wingbeat phases and correctly proportioned bodies, clear silhouettes against a pale cold blue-lavender sky. The formation becomes smaller into the distance. Their sense of a last call is conveyed by spacing, open atmosphere and receding flight, never graphic sound symbols. The fishermen's warmth and the geese's chill inhabit the same single evening.

Composition: generous horizontal 1536x1024 canvas, elevated three-quarter wide viewpoint from a gently sloping shore. Low distant indigo mountain ridges and reeds nearly dissolve into mist. A thin band of apricot-rose afterglow crosses the horizon; blue-gray autumn water occupies the broad middle and foreground. The boat's rippling wake makes one delicate curved lead-in toward the horizon, echoed only subtly by the arc of the geese above. Keep the sky and water spacious: quiet negative space carries the imagined sound. Distinct near/middle/far planes, small boat and human scale, no busy ornamental scenery. Do not combine two separate panels or place landmarks from separate regions next to each other. No large central pavilion, no giant moon, no fantasy palaces, no busy city.

Medium: exquisite contemporary Chinese ink-and-mineral-pigment landscape on subtly textured silk, with the spatial legibility and rich atmosphere of a hand-painted cinematic background. Refined confident brush edges on the boat and reeds, broader translucent washes over water and distant hills. Deep muted teal, blue-gray, lavender dusk, warm sienna and a narrow subdued gold reflection. Elegant and layered rather than faded or muddy. Rich enough in the foreground to invite close viewing; restrained enough to feel expansive and autumn-cold. Peaceful, wistful, dignified, sonorous.

No words, Chinese characters, calligraphy, poetry typography, captions, numerals, red seals, signatures, logos, watermarks or border. Do not reproduce an existing painting, book cover, film frame, named artist or studio style. Adult figure in opaque practical historical clothing, no sexualization, weapons, injury or distress. Credible boat construction and secure seated pose, plausible hands and oar, coherent reflections, anatomically legible geese. Native complete landscape composition, no crop or enlargement.
