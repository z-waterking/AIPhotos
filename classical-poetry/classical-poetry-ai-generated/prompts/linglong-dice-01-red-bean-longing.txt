Create one original Chinese poetic painting inspired by the classical image of a red love-seed enclosed deep within an exquisitely pierced die: a quiet longing kept inside, with no answer from the absent beloved. Landscape 1536x1024. No written words anywhere.

Medium: refined gongbi linework and softly layered Chinese mineral pigments and watercolor on delicate warm silk, with subtle ink diffusion and breathable unpainted atmosphere. This must feel like a deeply moving painted scene, with clear hand-painted material and light, rather than a photographed jewelry advertisement or a 3D product render.

Composition: intimate, low oblique view across an old dark wooden windowsill in a quiet Jiangnan room at blue hour. In the lower-right third rests ONE small, perfectly legible, warm milky-white jade die. Show a convincing rounded cube with three visible faces; its delicately carved circular pip openings pierce through to a common hollow interior. The die occupies about one fifth of the image width, the principal sharply observed subject. One single natural, glossy deep vermilion red love-seed is enclosed inside this hollow die, unmistakably visible through the nearest round opening, partly enclosed by the thick pale jade wall. The seed has a natural oval bean shape and a tiny pale hilum, never a heart-shaped gemstone. The jade is softly translucent at its edges, matte and time-worn elsewhere; the red seed is visibly inside, not painted onto the die, not balanced on top and not replacing its pips. A very fine, subdued shadow anchors it on the wooden sill.

Beyond the intimate foreground, a half-open old wooden lattice window at the far right frames a broad expanse of cool mist, faint still river water and a few very distant dissolving rooflines. The left two-thirds breathe with silver-blue empty atmosphere and softly fading ink shapes. Keep background architecture extremely restrained and the window consistent in perspective. A faint interrupted reflection on the distant river suggests separation and waiting without depicting people. The distance should feel quiet and unreachable.

Lighting and color: a thin slant of the last warm daylight enters from outside and touches the pierced jade and the enclosed red seed; its muted vermilion warmth is the only saturated color in the entire painting. Let the red be sensed softly through the adjacent jade wall, physically restrained, with no magical beam or glowing aura. Cool celadon, misty blue-gray, pale ivory, weathered charcoal-brown wood. Gentle contrast, rich but delicate detail at the die, softly dissolving distance.

Mood: intimate, tender, restrained, wistful and patient. One simple poetic visual idea: the red seed sheltered so deeply in white jade that its warmth seems held within the material itself. A sense of a real keepsake touched often, now left alone at the window.
