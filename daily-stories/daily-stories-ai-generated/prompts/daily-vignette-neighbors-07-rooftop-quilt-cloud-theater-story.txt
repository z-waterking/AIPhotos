被子上的云
原创虚构故事。

女孩跟爸爸上公共晒台看云，常看的那角天空今天被大白被子挡住了。她踮踮脚，还是看不全，只好站回爸爸身边，捏住他的衣角。

邻居刚夹好被子，看见她的样子，弯腰捡起一片干净落叶。叶子举到阳光里，被面上便多了一条长长的影子，尾巴一动，竟像条小鱼。

女孩立刻松开衣角，举起两根手指。爸爸帮她稳住手腕，一只小兔子也出现在被子上。邻居慢慢移叶子，鱼从兔子耳朵旁边游了过去。

傍晚被子收好，天空重新露出来。女孩指着一小条白云，请爸爸看看，刚才的鱼是不是游到那里了。邻居抱着白被子站在旁边，干净的叶子还搁在最上面，没有马上拿走。

The girl turns toward the shadow with delighted attention, and her father crouches slightly beside her to share the view. The quilt remains firmly clipped on the line, serving as a bright soft screen in the middle of the safe terrace.

Her father steadies her wrist lightly to help the shape stay clear, while the neighbor holds the leaf a little farther along the cloth. All three focus on the playful shadow meeting, smiling naturally. Use one visible arrangement of shadows rather than multiple motion stages. 4. 被子收好了，她还在云里找那条小鱼。
The neighbor now carries the neatly folded white quilt against her chest while father and daughter stand beside the cleared clothesline. The girl points toward a small elongated white cloud in the open blue sky, and both adults follow her gaze with amused smiles. The leaf rests on the folded quilt's top edge, connecting the earlier shadow play to this final moment. Keep the terrace walls high and the group centered safely within the space. The cloud may gently suggest a fish without becoming a literal animal or magical transformation.
