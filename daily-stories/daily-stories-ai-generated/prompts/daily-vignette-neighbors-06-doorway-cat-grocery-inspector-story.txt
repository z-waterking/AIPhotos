门口检查员
原创虚构故事。

阿叔买菜回来，总在对门慢一下。大橘猫早坐在那里，鼻子一伸，就要检查他布袋里的东西。主人笑着说，它今天又来上班了。

阿叔试着拎起一把青菜，猫却把头偏开，眼睛盯住袋底那只沙沙响的纸袋。菜拿走了，它还是看，连尾巴尖都抬得很认真。

主人找来一只干净空纸袋，没有提手，口也宽，放在门内的小垫子上。橘猫立即钻进去，身子慢慢消失，最后只剩粗尾巴在外面晃了一圈。

第二天，阿叔又带菜经过。纸袋口忽然探出一颗橘脑袋，照旧闻一闻他的布袋。检查完，它缩回自己的小地方。阿叔没再找青菜给它看，只朝袋口轻轻点了点头。

Show the vegetable and bag separated clearly enough to reveal the preference. The owner places a clean wide paper bag without handles on a small mat just inside her open doorway. The orange cat has already entered headfirst, leaving only its thick striped tail and hind paws visible at the broad opening. The older neighbor stands beside his groceries with a delighted grin. 4. 第二天，它从自己的小办公室探出了头。
The following morning, the orange cat peeks out from the same open paper bag on its doorway mat as the older neighbor passes with his cloth grocery tote. He pauses and tilts the tote slightly toward the cat's nose for their familiar inspection. The owner leans against the inner doorframe with a smile, letting the small ritual continue. The cat's head and white chin are visible while its body stays comfortably inside the bag. Keep the corridor clear and the bag entirely within the doorway, making the new arrangement both practical and playful.
