一杯水的办法
原创虚构故事。

邻居出门几天，请小许照看窗边三盆植物。他很认真，每次拿大杯子倒一样多的水，倒完还把盆外擦一圈。

过了两天，多肉的土还湿，蕨叶那盆却已经浅浅发干。小许蹲着看了半天。邻居回来后，没笑他，只找出一只小杯和几颗圆石子。

她在三盆旁分别放好不同数量的石子，再示范一小杯该怎么沿边慢慢倒。小许跟着试，忽然觉得三盆绿东西也像各有各的饭量，不能用同一只大碗招待。

下次帮忙，杯子和石子都在原位。灰猫坐到空花盆旁，仰头看着他。小许也看了它一会儿，另外端来一碗清水。猫低头喝起来，三盆植物则安安静静等各自的那一份。

1. 三盆植物交给他，他认真倒了同样一杯水。
In the neighbor's bright sitting room, Xu stands before a low plant stand holding a large plain cup of water. The three distinct pots contain a jade succulent, a fern, and a leafy vine. He has just poured water into one pot and looks carefully toward the others, trying to be conscientious. A gray tabby watches from the floor nearby. Keep the soil surfaces visible but the plants healthy, establishing a well-meant uniform approach rather than a disaster. Soft window light makes the different leaf shapes clear.

2. 有的土还湿着，有的却干得更快。
Xu crouches at the plant stand and compares the dark damp soil of the jade succulent with the lighter dry surface around the fern. He holds his empty large cup loosely in one hand, brows lifted in mild uncertainty. The neighbor, newly returned, stands beside him with a small travel bag and a calm reassuring smile. The vine in its blue pot remains healthy between the two contrasting examples. Use visible soil color and attentive gestures to show the practical puzzle, without wilted leaves or any alarming damage.

3. 小杯配上几颗石子，每盆都有自己的份。
The neighbor demonstrates with a much smaller measuring cup, pouring a modest amount at the fern's soil edge while Xu watches closely. Beside each pot sit a different small number of smooth colored pebbles, serving as simple visual reminders. Keep the arrangement limited and clear: one pale pebble near the succulent, two blue pebbles near the vine, and three ochre pebbles near the fern. No written labels are needed. Their hands and the little cup occupy the foreground, with the plants upright and refreshed in the window light.

4. 猫也来排队，他给它另放一碗清水。
On a later visit, Xu has the small measuring cup ready beside the plant stand and the colored pebbles remain neatly grouped by each pot. The gray tabby sits expectantly beside an empty decorative pot on the floor. Xu smiles and places a separate shallow bowl of fresh water on the floor beside the cat, away from the plants and soil. The neighbor watches from the doorway with her travel bag. Keep the cat's white paws and chest visible, and make the separate drinking bowl the gentle comic addition to his now-organized care routine.
